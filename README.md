# Food Watercolor Study

A Codex skill for turning food and drink photos into airy watercolor food-diary illustrations, paired with a short food-linked English fortune.

It preserves the recognizable prepared dish—its cooking state, key pairings, and serving relationship—while removing the original setting and simplifying repeated detail. Layouts vary deliberately, so food and text do not always appear in the same positions.

## What it creates

- Warm ivory paper, translucent watercolor washes, and restrained hand-painted outlines.
- A simplified but recognizable prepared dish or drink.
- One to three optional ingredient or cutaway details.
- A calm, original English fortune placed in the composition's remaining negative space.

## Use

Upload a food or drink photo and ask Codex to use `$food-watercolor-study`. The skill identifies the dish, selects an appropriate layout, creates the watercolor illustration, then overlays a precise, readable fortune.
